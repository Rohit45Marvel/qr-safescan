QR-SafeScan

Cross-Platform Mobile QR Code Phishing Detection System

A privacy-preserving mobile security application developed at EPITA to protect users from malicious QR codes using real-time threat intelligence and advanced URL analysis techniques.

Key Features

⚡

Real-Time Threat Intelligence

Instantly analyzes QR code URLs against live threat databases (Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, OTX/AlienVault) and security feeds to identify malicious content before you access it.

🔒

Zero-Trace Privacy

Complete privacy protection: no user accounts, no tracking, and no scan history storage. All analysis runs locally on your device—your data stays yours.

🛡️

Comprehensive Local Security Checks

Performs 27+ advanced on-device security checks, including phishing detection, typosquatting, suspicious TLDs, brand impersonation, and more, for robust protection even offline.

🚦

Smart “Traffic Light” Verdicts

Easy-to-understand, color-coded results (green/yellow/red) help you make instant, informed decisions about URL safety.

🎭

Advanced Deception Detection

Sophisticated algorithms detect typosquatting, homograph attacks, and other domain spoofing techniques used in phishing and scams.

📱

Cross-Platform & Offline Protection

Seamless security across iOS and Android devices, with full offline capability for local checks—ensuring you’re protected wherever you scan QR codes, even without internet.

How It Works

Follow these simple steps to protect yourself from malicious QR codes with QR-SafeScan.

1

📱 Download & Open Expo Go

First, download the Expo Go app from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS). Then scan our project QR code to launch QR-SafeScan instantly on your device.

Expo Go Required
2

📷 Scan the QR Code

Point your camera at any QR code. The app automatically detects and analyzes the embedded URL using real-time threat intelligence and advanced security algorithms.

3

✅ Safe Result

When the URL is verified as safe, you'll see a green "Safe" verdict. The app confirms the link is legitimate and you can proceed with confidence.

✅ URL Verified Safe
4

⚠️ Suspicious URL

When a URL shows uncertain signals, the app displays a yellow "Suspicious" warning. Multiple security APIs couldn't fully verify the link — proceed with caution or avoid it entirely.

⚠️ Suspicious - Use Caution
5

🚨 Threat Detected

If a malicious or phishing URL is detected, the app immediately warns you with a red alert. The threat is blocked before you can access the dangerous content.

🚨 Phishing Attempt Blocked
